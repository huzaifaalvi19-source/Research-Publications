# Manufacturing Research

Research on the relationship between machining method, microstructure, and tooling performance.

## Featured publication

**Proportional Analysis and Microstructural Characterization of Threading Dies Manufactured Using Conventional and EDM Machining Methods**

The study compares H13 threading dies produced by conventional machining and electrical discharge machining (EDM), using hardness testing, microstructural examination, and threading trials.

## Research question

How does the manufacturing route affect a threading die's material condition and performance under the study's test conditions?

| Aspect | Study focus |
| --- | --- |
| Material | H13 die steel |
| Manufacturing routes | Conventional machining and EDM |
| Characterization | Hardness variation and microstructure |
| Performance assessment | Threading trials on mild-steel rods |

## Reported findings

- The conventionally machined die failed after **16 inches** of threading.
- The EDM-fabricated die threaded **up to 20 inches** under the experimental conditions.
- The study reports less hardness variation and greater microstructural stability for the EDM-fabricated die.

These results describe the specific experiments; they are not a universal tool-life guarantee for all machining conditions.
